Questions before booking?

Frequently asked questions

Can I customize a package?

Yes. In the production version, users can change hotel category, number of days, transport, activities and meal plans.

Can I book only a hotel or transport?

Yes. A modular checkout can allow package, hotel, transfer, activity or complete-trip booking.

Will I be able to pay online?

The demo does not process payments. A production build can integrate Razorpay or another payment provider.

Can I track my booking?

Yes. Add a traveller dashboard with booking status, invoices, itinerary, support and cancellation/refund tracking.
